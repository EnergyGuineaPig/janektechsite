ABOUT

Hosting a private website with no limits

onionWeb is a lightweight Windows application that lets you create and host a Tor Onion website without complex configuration. It automatically sets up PHP, configures Tor, generates your .onion address, and provides an easy-to-use interface for managing your hidden service.
