LOCAL TESTING

Your server. Your controls.

  1. Choose your project.Open the local testing window and select your website folder.
  2. Configure your server.Choose the server, executable paths, port and testing options. Installed runtimes are detected; missing PHP can be downloaded and verified automatically.
  3. Click Start server.Opening the controls, detecting a runtime or finishing setup leaves the server stopped. Start, open in your browser and stop whenever you choose.

Python's local server is used for static files only. PHP projects use PHP or an Apache installation configured for PHP.
